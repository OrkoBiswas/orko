# Product requirements

## Goal

Convince qualified clients to hire Orko Biswas by proving craft through a memorable portfolio library and by making a serious inquiry easy to submit.

## Audiences

- Brands and founders needing campaign visuals
- Agencies seeking a reliable creative production partner
- Creators needing YouTube, short-form, and social systems
- Recruiters and studios evaluating skill, process, and fit

## Critical journeys

1. Discover Orko's positioning, availability, and strongest work within the first viewport.
2. Search and filter a large project library without relying on hover.
3. Open a project case study and understand challenge, craft, process, and deliverables.
4. Compare services and start a detailed project brief.
5. Submit an inquiry with clear validation, consent, reference number, and durable storage.
6. Sign in as owner, review inquiries, and manage project publication state.

## Success criteria

Fast first rendering, clear conversion paths, accessible interaction, persistent backend records, no fake claims or testimonials, production build success, and truthful fallbacks when external services are not configured.
